A portfolio starts
with paying attention.

Keep a sketchbook. Take apart an everyday problem. Document a material test, a repair or an object you have made. Show what changed between your first idea and your next attempt.

For advanced entry, bring evidence of the relevant foundations: patterns and garments for digital fashion, or design/technical projects for interaction and physical computing.

Before you apply.

Is this a degree programme?

The current proposal is for institute certificate programmes. No university affiliation or degree equivalence is claimed. Confirmed awarding arrangements will be published before enrolment.

When will courses start, and what will they cost?

Start dates and fees are being finalised. The website will be updated with the confirmed schedule, fee inclusions and payment/refund terms before students are asked to enrol.

Do I need a polished portfolio to apply?

The proposed core admissions process values observation, curiosity and willingness to make. Simple sketches, experiments and personal projects can help show your thinking. Advanced programmes require relevant prior learning.

Will there be industry experience?

The proposed core programmes include 12 weeks of supervised industry work. The one-year full-time pathways include 8 weeks. A placement or externally mentored live studio brief is planned; specific partners and placements are not yet confirmed.

Are jobs or placements guaranteed?

No. The programmes are designed around professional readiness, practical work and portfolio development. Employment depends on individual performance, opportunities and employer requirements.

Are these courses for children?

The main programmes are college-level pathways. School-age workshops will be separate and scheduled in spare studio time.

Can I submit my registration now?

The registration section is being prepared for the school’s CRM form. Until that integration is connected, the on-page form is a preview only and does not submit or store your information.
